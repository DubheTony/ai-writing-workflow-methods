# An AI Agent-Assisted Writing Workflow Inspired by Boosting or Pipelines

[中文](README.md) | [English](README.en.md)

This repository documents AI-assisted writing methods used in practice.

The main idea is to combine multiple Skills within a workflow, using your own texts, tools, and constraints to produce writing that matches your style.

## Data and Source Boundaries

The project uses only rewritten fictional examples and general methods.

The figures in the quantitative sections are fictional illustrations.

Several third-party writing Skills are used. Their names, source links, and verification dates are recorded. Where available, upstream commits, versions, and licenses are also recorded; otherwise, they are marked as unknown. Their prompts are not copied.
